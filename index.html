JohnDoe

Note: this app was created solely for testing the Meta API and will eventually be deleted. It is not intended for public use.

A project of a private individual (an IT professional learning how Meta for Developers works), not of an organisation or a company.

JohnDoe is a tool for creating and managing ads on Meta (Facebook, Instagram) through the Marketing API. The app is in a testing phase and serves only its creators' own ad accounts. It does not store anyone else's data: it works only on data from the private account of the Meta app owner and people they have authorised to take part in the tests.
